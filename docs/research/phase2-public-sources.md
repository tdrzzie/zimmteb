Inspected 21 September 2026. Scope: all eight domains, English, Shona, Northern
Ndebele and future code-switch tracks. **No rows are dataset admission approvals.**
These links are public source candidates; no article text is bundled in this inventory.

## Reuse evidence

The inspected Wikimedia text pages link to CC BY-SA 4.0. The
[Wikimedia terms, section 7](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use#7._Licensing_of_Content)
describe text reuse, attribution, imported-content notices and modifications.
The [CC BY-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
summarizes credit, change notices and share-alike conditions. Keep source/history
links and license notices, review page-specific notices and exclude images unless
their individual licenses are checked. Do not relicense extracted or adapted text
as the project's CC0 fixture. Store a separate licensed dataset manifest.

License eligibility does not establish factual quality, local representativeness,
privacy clearance, or native-speaker validation. At admission, retrieve the exact
revision, verify its notices and preserve a byte checksum of the extracted passage.

## Shona and Northern Ndebele

- Shona agriculture: [Kurima, revision 106136](https://sn.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Kurima&oldid=106136).
- Shona education: [Dzidzo, revision 88982](https://sn.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Dzidzo&oldid=88982).
- Northern Ndebele discovery: [Wikimedia Incubator Wp/nd](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/nd)
  explicitly identifies North Ndebele, ISO 639-1 `nd`, ISO 639-3 `nde`, as an open
  test wiki. [Its main page](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/nd/Main_Page)
  is a discovery starting point. This is not Southern Ndebele (`nbl`/`nr`) or Zulu.

Shona and Incubator footer license links were followed to CC BY-SA 4.0. The two
Shona articles have not been linguistically reviewed for this project and are not
assumed to be equivalent to the Zimbabwe-specific English pages. No domain-specific
Northern Ndebele passage or natural code-switch corpus has been admitted yet.

## Broader local sources

The [Zimbabwe foreign ministry agriculture page](https://www.zimfa.gov.zw/index.php/about-us/zimbabwe-in-brief/agriculture)
is a relevant local discovery lead, but no redistribution license was established
during this pass. It is not cleared for copying. Apply the same rule to public bank,
telecom, education and government pages: record explicit reuse rights or obtain
permission before dataset admission. Public visibility alone is insufficient.

The next intake pass should prioritize locally relevant, openly licensed material
over filling coverage cells with generic prose. No scraping job, reviewer contact,
dataset publication or automatic approval was performed by this discovery pass.
