# Possible volunteer recruitment routes

Checked 21 September 2026. These are discovery routes, not recruited reviewers,
endorsements or consent to receive bulk messages. No invitations have been sent to
these organizations or communities. The project has published its own
[GitHub recruitment issue](https://github.com/tdrzzie/zimmteb/issues/1).

- [Northern Ndebele Wikimedia Incubator](https://incubator.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wp/nd):
  the project page invites knowledgeable language contributors. Follow its community
  discussion norms before proposing a small voluntary review activity.
- [Shona Wikipedia](https://sn.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurima): use its community/help
  links to identify the appropriate discussion venue; do not message every editor
  or assume authors are available for this project.
- [NUST Faculty of Communication and Information Science contact page](https://www.nust.ac.zw/cis/index.php/contact):
  a public institutional route for asking whether there is an appropriate language
  or student community to approach. The site lists `journalism@nust.ac.zw`; this
  is a referral lead, not evidence that the department can supply qualified reviewers.
  The [journalism programme page](https://www.nust.ac.zw/cis/index.php/departments/department-of-journalism-and-media-studies)
  mentions Ndebele/Shona in entry requirements, which does not establish individual expertise.

Start with the [volunteer invitation](volunteer-invitation.md), a small optional
batch and clear licensing/attribution terms. Do not imply a partnership or promise
payment. Confirm the destination and message before sending on the owner's behalf.
