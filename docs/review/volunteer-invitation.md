# Volunteer invitation

Published on 21 September 2026 as [recruitment issue #1](https://github.com/tdrzzie/zimmteb/issues/1).

## Help review a small Zimbabwean-language retrieval dataset

ZimMTEB is an open-source project studying how search and text embedding models
handle English, chiShona and Northern isiNdebele in Zimbabwean settings.

We are looking for volunteers comfortable reading and writing chiShona or Northern
isiNdebele, alongside English, to review a small draft pilot. Northern isiNdebele
is distinct from Southern Ndebele and Zulu; please tell us which variety you use.

The pilot contains short public-source excerpts and AI-drafted questions, including
artificial code switching. None has been approved by a project speaker reviewer.
We need help identifying language errors, unnatural wording, ambiguous questions
and incorrect or missing relevant-document labels. You can flag an item as uncertain
or unsuitable rather than correcting it, and you do not need programming experience.

You may choose a small batch. We will agree on its size and review process before
you begin. This is an unpaid volunteer request with no minimum time commitment.
Participation is optional, and you can stop at any time. We will explain attribution
and the proposed public license for any accepted corrections before accepting them.

Please do not send personal records, private conversations, customer information
or copyrighted material you do not have permission to share. Public attribution
can use a pseudonym; private contact details do not belong in the dataset.

If interested, open an issue titled **Volunteer language review** at
https://github.com/tdrzzie/zimmteb/issues and say which language(s) and variety you
can review. No personal contact details, age, identity documents or location are
needed in that public issue. We will arrange the next step before sharing work.

Project: https://github.com/tdrzzie/zimmteb
